Welcome to Golden Hills

Where Algerian Heritage Meets Contemporary Elegance

Nestled in the heart of Sétif, Hotel Golden Hills is a sanctuary of refined comfort and timeless luxury. Our newly renovated rooms blend traditional Algerian warmth with modern sophistication, offering an intimate residential atmosphere unlike any other.


Just steps from Sétif Railway Station and the iconic Kawthar Mosque, our boutique property invites you to experience the city's rich heritage while enjoying world-class amenities, exquisite dining, and personalized service that anticipates your every need.
